How to Get More Impressions on X (9 Steps That Work in 2026)

By Prashant Sharma · Oct 26, 2026 · 6 min read

How do you get more impressions on X?

To get more impressions on X, post things your followers will react to in the first hours, because the For You feed ranks posts by predicted likes, replies and reposts. Reply early to bigger accounts in your niche, stick to one or two topics, post most days, and avoid anything people mute or report.

Here's a moment most people on X know. You write a post you're proud of, hit send, and check back an hour later. It says 41 views. Meanwhile a half-finished thought from someone else is sitting at 80,000.

That gap isn't luck. It comes from how X decides who sees what. The good news is that most of what drives impressions is in your hands, and none of it needs a big budget.

What counts as an impression on X?

An impression on X is one time your post is shown to someone. The view count under each post works almost the same way: according to X's help page on view counts, any logged-in person who views your post counts, wherever they see it.

A few details matter here. Your own views count too. The same person seeing your post on their phone and then on their laptop can count twice. Posts embedded on other websites don't add views.

So impressions measure exposure, not attention. That's why they move fast. Getting more of them is mostly about getting your post into more feeds, more often. If you want the full breakdown of the two numbers, I wrote a separate guide on impressions vs engagement.

How does the X algorithm decide who sees your post?

The X algorithm predicts how likely each viewer is to like, reply, repost, share, or block your post, then turns those predictions into one score. Posts with the highest scores fill the For You feed. That's the core of it.

X published this system on GitHub. The open-source For You algorithm pulls posts from two places: accounts you follow (in-network) and accounts you don't (out-of-network). Both get ranked by the same model.

Three details from that code are worth knowing:

  • Posts older than 48 hours are filtered out of For You. Your post has a short window.
  • Out-of-network posts get a discount. Reaching strangers is harder than reaching followers.
  • Each extra post from the same author gets a decaying score in a viewer's feed. Ten posts in an hour won't all show up.

There's also a "new-author boost" that lifts posts from authors whose impressions are below a threshold. X hasn't said what that threshold is. For the bigger picture, see how the X algorithm works in 2026.

The 9 steps to get more impressions on X

These steps follow directly from how ranking works. Start with the first three. They make the biggest difference for most accounts.

1. Write a first line that earns the stop

People decide in a second whether to keep reading. Lead with the result, the surprise, or the question. Cut the warm-up. Use the free post length checker if you tend to run long.

2. Give people a reason to reply

Replies show the algorithm that a post started a conversation. Ask a real question, share an opinion people can push back on, or leave a gap they can fill. Then answer the replies you get. That back-and-forth keeps the post alive.

3. Reply early to bigger accounts

This is the simplest change with the biggest payoff for small accounts. A thoughtful reply on a fresh post from a larger account in your niche can be seen by thousands. X's For you timeline help page says reply ranking considers whether the original author replied and whether the reply comes from someone the viewer follows. My guide on writing replies that add value goes deeper.

4. Pick one or two topics and stay there

Out-of-network reach depends on X matching your posts to people with the same interests. If you post about coding on Monday and football on Tuesday, that match gets blurry. Consistency helps the system learn who your posts are for.

5. Post when your audience is awake

Because the For You window is about 48 hours, and early reactions feed the ranking, timing matters. Post when your followers are online so the first reactions come fast. The best time to post on X guide covers how to find your own window.

6. Use threads for ideas that deserve space

A thread gives people more to read and more places to reply. Keep the first post strong enough to stand alone. The free thread maker splits a long draft into clean posts.

7. Space out your posts

Since each extra post from you in a viewer's feed gets a lower score, spreading posts across the day usually beats posting in bursts.

8. Avoid what triggers negative signals

The ranking model also predicts "not interested," mutes, blocks and reports. Those push your score down. Engagement bait, off-topic hashtag stuffing and repetitive self-promotion invite exactly those reactions.

9. Re-share your best posts

A post that did well once often does well again for people who missed it. Quote it with a new angle a few weeks later.

Does X Premium get you more impressions?

Premium appears to help reach, but it isn't magic. Buffer analyzed 18.8 million posts and found Premium accounts got around 10 times the median reach of regular accounts. X's own Premium page lists "reply prioritization" as a feature at every paid tier, and says replies from verified accounts get "a slight preference."

Here's the thing, though. Buffer's data shows a link, not proof that paying causes the gap. Premium users may also be more active. I'd treat Premium as an amplifier for good posts, not a fix for weak ones. One more thing to know: since October 12, 2026, Premium is part of The X Subscription, which runs from Plus at $8 a month to Ultra at $200. Existing Premium plans keep working.

What this means for you

If you only change three habits this week, make them these: sharper first lines, early replies on bigger accounts, and posting at the right time. Each one works with the algorithm instead of against it.

It also helps to see which accounts are worth replying to. I built a free extension, XVision, that shows a "Reply early" badge on fresh posts from bigger accounts that still have few replies. It's one way to spot those chances faster.

When you want to stretch beyond impressions, read about improving your post reach or increasing views on a single post.

More impressions come from better habits, not hacks

Getting more impressions on X is simpler than it sounds. Write posts people want to react to, show up where conversations are already happening, and give the algorithm a clear idea of who you're for. Do that for a few weeks and the numbers usually follow. You've got this.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many impressions is good on X?

A good impression count on X depends on your follower count and niche. Buffer's 2024 to 2025 study found regular accounts typically got under 100 impressions per post, while Premium accounts got around 600. Compare each post to your own average, not to viral accounts, and aim to raise that average month by month.

Do hashtags increase impressions on X?

Hashtags can help a little on X, mainly by making posts easier to find in search. X's help center recommends using no more than 2 hashtags per post. Stuffing many unrelated hashtags can look like spam, which invites mutes and reports and can lower the reach of a post.

Why do impressions stop growing after a day or two?

Impressions on X slow down after a day or two because the For You algorithm filters out posts older than 48 hours, according to X's open-source code. After that, new views mostly come from your profile, search, links and reposts. That is why the first hours after posting matter so much.

Do replies count toward my impressions?

Yes, replies are posts too, so each reply you write collects its own views and impressions on X. A reply on a large account's fresh post can reach far more people than an original post from a small account. Your analytics may show replies separately, depending on which view you open.

Does deleting and reposting a post get more impressions?

No, deleting and reposting the same post on X usually resets its likes and replies, which are the signals that help it spread. A better option is to quote your own earlier post with a fresh angle. X's help center confirms you can repost or quote your own older posts when they become relevant again.
