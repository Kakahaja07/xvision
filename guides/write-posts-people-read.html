Write posts that don't sound like AI slop

The template phrases people have learned to scroll past, and how to write posts that sound like a real person.

X is full of posts that all sound the same: a big promise, an emoji, a list of short lines, and words like "game-changer". Readers have learned to scroll past them. Posts that sound like a real person stand out more than ever.

Phrases people scroll past

These show up so often in templated and AI-written posts that many readers skip anything containing them:

  • "Let's dive in", "Here's the kicker", "Buckle up"
  • "Game-changer", "Unlock the power of", "Elevate your"
  • "In today's fast-paced world"
  • "Most people don't know this", "Nobody talks about this"
  • A list where every line starts with ✅ or 👉
XVision: the AI slop filter collapses posts that stack these patterns, so your feed stays readable. It is pattern matching, not mind reading, and every hidden post keeps a Show button.

What to do instead

Be specific

Vague
"Consistency is the secret to growth 🚀"
Specific
"I replied to 20 posts a day for a month. Followers went from 80 to 410. Most came from 3 replies."

Numbers, names, dates and details make a post believable and memorable.

Write the way you talk

Read your post out loud. If you would never say it to a friend, rewrite it. Short sentences are good; a staccato list of fragments is not.

Share something only you could write

Your own mistakes, numbers and opinions are what nobody else can copy. A generic tip can come from anyone. "Here is what broke when we launched" can only come from you.

Lead with the point

Put the most interesting line first. Skip "So I've been thinking..." and open with the thought.

Engagement bait to avoid

"Drop your link below", "Like if you agree", "Who else...?" may get replies, but they attract low-quality engagement and many people mute or filter them. Ask real questions you want answered instead.

A quick checklist before you post

  1. Does the first line make someone want to read the second?
  2. Is there at least one specific detail?
  3. Would you say it out loud?
  4. Did you cut every word that adds nothing?
