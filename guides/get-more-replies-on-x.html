How to Get More Replies on X (Without Begging for Them)

By Prashant Sharma · Oct 26, 2026 · 6 min read

How do you get more replies on X?

To get more replies on X, ask questions people can answer from their own experience, take a clear position, and reply to every early comment yourself. As of October 2026, X's open-source ranking code weights a reply at 5.0 versus 0.66 for a like, so replies are worth the effort.

I used to think people didn't reply because they had nothing to say. Then I looked at my own feed. I scroll past dozens of posts I agree with and only stop to reply when a post asks me something, or says something I want to push back on. Most posts give readers no door to walk through.

A reply is a response written under someone's post. It takes more effort than a like, so it signals stronger interest. That is why replies matter on X: they show the post started a conversation, and conversations keep a post alive longer in people's feeds.

Why replies matter more than likes

Replies matter more than likes because they show real interest and carry more weight in X's ranking code. As of October 2026, X's current weight file gives a reply 5.0 and a like 0.66.

Those numbers come from X's current ranking weights on GitHub, in the xai-org/x-algorithm repo X first published in January 2026. The file changes often, so treat it as a snapshot. One detail is worth knowing. Since July 2026 the code has a mutual-follow reply boost, set to 15 as of October 2026, which raises the weight on the chance you reply to a post from someone you follow who follows you back. In plain words, replies between people who know each other count extra.

The older 2023 release pointed the same way, with 13.5 for a reply, 0.5 for a like and 75.0 when the author engaged with a reply. That author weight doesn't appear in the 2026 file, but X's help center still says reply ranking looks at whether the original author replied.

If you care about your overall numbers, our guide to increasing your Twitter engagement rate covers the bigger picture. This one is only about replies.

8 habits that get more replies on X

The habits that get more replies on X all lower the effort it takes to respond. People reply when they know what to say, feel invited to say it, and expect someone to read it.

1. Ask a question with an easy answer

"What do you think?" is hard to answer. "What's the first tool you install on a new laptop?" is easy. A good question has a short, personal answer that nobody can get wrong. Need ideas? Here's a list of questions that start conversations on X.

2. Take a clear stance

Fence-sitting posts get nods. A clear, honest opinion gets replies from people who agree and people who don't. You don't need to be harsh. "I think most people post too often, not too little" is clear enough to start a debate.

3. Share something unfinished

Show a draft, a landing page, two logo options or a half-made plan and ask which way to go. People love helping with a real decision. This works especially well for founders who build in public on X.

4. Leave room in the post

If your post covers every angle, there is nothing left to add. Give three tips and ask for a fourth. Share your answer and ask for theirs. That gap is where replies go.

5. Reply to every early reply

Here's the good news: this is the habit with the biggest payoff, and it's free. Answer the first replies quickly and with substance. X's help page on how conversations work says reply ranking considers "if the original post author has replied." Your answers lift those replies, and people notice that you read them.

6. Keep replies open

X lets you limit who can reply: everyone, accounts you follow, accounts you mention, or verified accounts. If you want more replies, check that a post isn't set to a narrow option by accident. You can change the setting even after posting.

7. Post when your followers are around

A question posted while your audience sleeps gets answered by nobody. Find your active hours and post your conversation starters then. Our best time to post on X guide explains how to find yours.

8. Reply to others first

Replies are a two-way street. If you add value under other people's posts, they are far more likely to show up under yours. The guide on how to write replies that add value covers this in detail.

What should you avoid when chasing replies?

Avoid engagement bait like "Drop your link below" or "Reply with a 🔥 if you agree." It can bring quick numbers, but the replies are empty. Based on what I've seen, it also makes thoughtful readers take you less seriously.

X's own authenticity policy also bans coordinating to exchange engagement, such as reply pods where a group agrees to reply to each other's posts. That is not a risk worth taking. Real questions beat bait, and they bring in people who stick around.

Also skip rage replies on purpose. Picking fights may pull in replies, but they come from people who will never follow you.

What this means for you

Getting more replies on X is not about tricks. It's about making it easy and worthwhile for someone to answer you. Ask real questions, take a stance, leave a gap, and show up in your own replies.

Start small. Pick one post this week, end it with a clear question, and answer every reply within the hour. Once you do this a few times, it becomes second nature. If your replies under other people's posts aren't being seen at all, check why your replies get no views first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do my posts get views but no replies?

Posts usually get views but no replies on X when they don't invite a response. A complete statement with no question, opinion or gap gives readers nothing to add. Ending with a specific question, or sharing a clear opinion people can agree or disagree with, gives them a reason to reply.

Should I reply to every comment on my posts?

Yes, replying to every genuine comment is worth it, especially on smaller accounts where each reply is easy to see. X's help center says reply ranking considers whether the original author replied, and a 2025 Buffer study linked creator replies to about 8% higher engagement on X. It also shows readers that their effort gets noticed.

Do polls get more replies than questions?

No, polls usually collect votes rather than replies on X. A poll can have up to four options and runs from 5 minutes to 7 days. Votes are private and easy, so many people vote and move on. Add a follow-up line like "Tell me why below" to turn votes into replies.

Is it okay to ask people to reply?

Yes, asking a genuine question and inviting answers is fine on X. What causes problems is bait, such as "Reply YES to get the link," and coordinated groups that agree to reply to each other. X's authenticity policy bans coordinated engagement exchanges, so keep requests honest and specific.

How fast should I reply to replies on X?

Aim to reply within the first hour after posting on X, while the post is fresh and people are still reading it. Early answers keep the conversation active and give others a reason to join. A late reply still helps the person who wrote to you, but it does less for the post's reach.
