How to Launch a Product on X: A Step-by-Step Launch-Day Plan

By Prashant Sharma · Oct 26, 2026 · 6 min read

How do you launch a product on X?

To launch a product on X, warm up your audience for two to four weeks, then post one clear launch thread with a short demo video, pin it, and spend the first few hours replying to every comment. Send personal messages to people who asked for the product, and post follow-up updates for a week.

A lot of launches on X go like this. The founder posts "We're live!" with a link, refreshes the page for an hour, sees 11 likes and goes back to fixing bugs. The product deserved better. The launch just had no runway and no plan for the hours after posting.

The good news is that a solid X launch isn't complicated. It's a few weeks of warm-up, one well-built thread and a day of being fully present in the replies. Let me walk you through it.

What should you do before launching a product on X?

Before launching a product on X, spend two to four weeks sharing progress, collecting the names of people who want it and preparing every asset in advance. Launch day should be about replying, not writing.

  • Share the build. Post screenshots, small decisions and a "launching soon" teaser. My guide on how to build in public on Twitter covers the rhythm.
  • Track interest. When someone replies "I want this," add them to a private List and note it. These are your first messages on launch day.
  • Prepare assets. A 30 to 60 second demo video, two or three screenshots, a landing page that matches the launch post and short answers to the questions you expect.
  • Fix your profile. Update your bio and get your launch post ready to pin. See how to create a good pinned post.

Keep the format limits in mind. According to X's help page on posting, a standard post holds up to 280 characters and up to four media items. Longer posts need a paid plan. You can check drafts with the free post length checker.

A step-by-step launch-day plan on X

A launch-day plan on X has five blocks: post and pin the thread, reply to everything for the first few hours, message the people who asked for it, share a second angle later in the day, and close with a thank-you post. Clear your calendar for it.

WhenWhat to do
Hour 0Post the launch thread and pin it to your profile
Hours 0 to 3Reply to every comment and quote post, with real answers
Hour 1Send personal DMs to people on your "want this" List
MiddayPost a second angle: one use case, one customer type or a short demo clip
EveningPost a thank-you with honest early feedback and what you'll fix next

Pick a launch time when your own followers are most active. My guide to the best time to post on X explains how to find it.

Why do replies matter so much? The open-source For You feed code from xAI ranks posts by predicting actions such as replies, reposts, profile clicks and how long people stay on a post. A launch post that sparks real conversation simply gives the ranking more to work with. That's also why you should never ask friends to mass-like it. X's authenticity rules ban coordinated engagement, and fake signals tell you nothing.

How do you write a launch thread that works?

A launch thread that works opens with the problem and a demo, explains what the product does in plain words, says who it's for and ends with one clear action. Four to six posts is usually enough.

  1. Post 1: the problem in one line, the product name and the demo video.
  2. Post 2: how it works, in three short steps.
  3. Post 3: who it's for, and who it isn't for.
  4. Post 4: what it costs, or that it's free.
  5. Post 5: the link and one ask, like "try it and tell me what breaks."

The thread maker tool helps split a draft into posts. For more on structure, see how to write Twitter threads people finish.

Example: launching a free Chrome extension on X

A free Chrome extension launch on X needs one extra thing: a dead-simple install path, because people decide in seconds. The demo should show the extension working on X itself, and the thread should explain installation step by step.

Take my own product as an example. XVision is a free Chrome extension that adds follower counts, badges and a growth dashboard to x.com. It isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so a launch thread for it has to explain clearly where to download it and how to install it. The demo is a short screen recording of the feed with badges appearing. The ask is simple: install it and tell me what's confusing. I'm not going to promise results I don't have. The point is the structure, which fits any free tool.

What should you do in the week after launch?

In the week after launch, keep the story going: share feedback, ship quick fixes in public and add updates to the launch thread. A launch loses momentum fast when the founder goes silent on day two.

X's help page on threads notes that you can add posts to a thread after publishing it. Use that to post day-two and day-five updates under the original launch, so anyone who finds it later sees the product improving. Then turn new followers into users with the steps in how to turn X followers into customers.

Launch day is a beginning, not a finish line

Learning how to launch a product on X comes down to preparation and presence. Warm up for a few weeks, post one clear thread, reply to everyone, message the people who asked and keep posting updates all week. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best day to launch a product on X?

The best day to launch a product on X is a day when your followers are active and you can spend hours replying. 2026 studies from Sprout Social and Buffer both rank Tuesday to Thursday among the strongest days, though they disagree on the best time. Test against your own audience.

Can you launch a product on X with no followers?

Yes, you can launch a product on X with no followers, but plan for a smaller first day. Spend the warm-up weeks replying to people in your niche so some of them recognize you. On launch day, share the link in replies only where it truly answers someone's question, never as spam.

Should you use X ads for a product launch?

X ads can help a product launch once the organic launch post shows people respond to it. X's advertising page describes its Amplify format as ideal for awareness and product launches, and says there is no minimum budget. Promoting the proven launch post is the simplest place to start.

How long should a product launch thread be on X?

On X, a product launch thread works best at about four to six posts. The first post needs the problem, the product name and a demo. Later posts cover how it works, who it is for and the price. Shorter threads keep the link close to the top, so more readers reach it.

Should you launch on X and other platforms on the same day?

Launching on X and other platforms on the same day can work if you can reply on all of them. Spreading too thin is the bigger risk. A practical approach is to launch on X first, where your audience already is, then share on other platforms over the following days with lessons from day one.
