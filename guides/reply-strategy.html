The reply-first playbook for X

Why replies grow small accounts faster than posts, which posts to reply to, and how to write replies people click.

If you take one thing from this site, take this: when your account is small, your replies are worth more than your posts. Here is why, and how to do it well.

Why replies work

A post from a small account is shown to a small audience. A reply under a popular post is shown to everyone reading that conversation, and those readers are already interested in the topic. A sharp reply gets likes, and people tap through to see who wrote it. That tap is where follows come from.

Which posts to reply to

Not every post is worth replying to. The best ones have three things in common:

  • Fresh: posted in the last 30 minutes. Early replies sit near the top; late ones get buried.
  • Quiet: fewer than about 20 replies so far. Your reply can still stand out.
  • Right-sized author: big enough to have an audience, small enough to read their replies. Roughly 5,000 to 100,000 followers is a good range.

How to write a reply people click

Weak
"So true! 🔥"
Strong
"Same here. We cut onboarding from 5 screens to 2 and sign-ups went up the next week."
  • Add a specific: a number, a short story, an example from your own work.
  • Disagree politely when you do: a thoughtful counterpoint often gets more attention than agreement.
  • Keep it short: one to three sentences. The reply is a trailer, your profile is the movie.
  • Answer the question: if the post asks something, actually answer it.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Replying to the biggest accounts only. Their replies are crowded, and they rarely read them.
  • Self-promotion in every reply. Mention your project only when it is truly relevant.
  • Copy-paste or AI-generated replies. People notice, and it hurts trust.
  • Arguing in bad faith. It may get views, but it attracts the wrong followers.

Find who ignores you, and who doesn't

After a few weeks, you will notice some accounts always reply back and some never do. Spend more time with the first group. They are becoming your network.

A simple daily target

Aim for 20 good replies a day. Track it for a week. Most people who try this see more profile visits within days, because each good reply is a small ad for your account.
