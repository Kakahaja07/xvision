How to Schedule Twitter Posts in Advance: Every Option in 2026

By Prashant Sharma · Oct 26, 2026 · 6 min read

How do I schedule Twitter posts in advance?

To schedule Twitter posts in advance, use X Pro's composer, which supports scheduled posts and a Scheduled column, or the Composer in ads.x.com. X lists X Pro as a Premium+ feature. Third-party tools such as Buffer and Typefully also schedule X posts and threads, and both offer free plans as of October 2026.

Picture this. Your audience is eight hours ahead, your best posting window is 6 a.m. their time, and you're asleep. Or it's a busy launch week and you just don't have a spare minute at noon. That's when scheduling stops being a nice-to-have.

Scheduling means writing a post now and setting it to publish later at a time you choose. On X (formerly Twitter), the options changed a lot over the last two years, and some now depend on which paid plan you have. Here's the good news: there's a route for every budget, including free ones.

Can you schedule posts natively on X in 2026?

Yes, X has native scheduling, but the documented routes are X Pro and the X Ads composer. Both run on the web, and X Pro needs a higher paid plan.

A quick note on plans. On October 12, 2026, X folded X Premium into a new bundle called The X Subscription. Existing Basic, Premium and Premium+ subscribers keep their plans. X's help pages still describe features by the older tier names, so that's how the options below are labeled.

Option 1: Schedule posts with X Pro

X Pro, the tool formerly known as TweetDeck, is X's main native scheduler. X's help page on how to use X Pro says it lets you "schedule posts for posting in the future," and it offers a Scheduled column that lists your queued posts.

X also says the new X Pro composer can create threads and add photos, videos, GIFs and polls, including in scheduled posts. The catch is access. At the time of writing (October 2026), X's Premium help page lists X Pro under "Only for Premium+ Tier." xAI's own docs say the Premium, Super and Ultra plans on The X Subscription include full X Premium+ benefits.

How to use it:

  1. Open X Pro on the web and log in with your X account.
  2. Write your post in the composer, adding media or a poll if you like.
  3. Choose the schedule option, then pick a date and time.
  4. Add a Scheduled column so you can see and manage everything in your queue.

One thing to know: X's help center says posts sent through X Pro can't be changed with the Edit post feature. Proofread before you schedule.

Option 2: Schedule posts in the X Ads composer

The Composer in ads.x.com can also schedule posts. It's built for advertisers, so it feels a little clunkier for everyday posting.

According to X's help page on scheduled posts in X Ads, the steps are:

  1. Log in to ads.x.com and open the Composer tab.
  2. Write your post and add media or cards.
  3. Click the dropdown arrow next to Post.
  4. Pick the date and time, then click Schedule.

The page mainly describes Promoted-only posts, so check how a scheduled post will appear before relying on it for organic posting. X's business composer page says you can publish, draft or schedule posts from it. It also notes that posts with conversation settings, such as limits on who can reply, can't be scheduled. You manage scheduled posts under the Posts tab by switching the filter to "Scheduled."

What about the regular x.com composer?

Some third-party guides report a schedule (calendar) icon in the desktop x.com composer for some accounts. X's Help Center doesn't document it at the time of writing, and reports disagree on who gets it.

So check your own composer on a desktop browser. If you see a calendar icon next to the media buttons, you can use it. If not, the options above and below are the reliable routes. I'd rather tell you that honestly than promise a button you might not have.

Option 3: Use a third-party scheduler

Third-party schedulers are the easiest route if you don't pay for a top X plan, and many handle threads and other networks too. I checked two popular ones on their own sites as of October 2026.

Which scheduling option should you pick?

Pick the option that matches your plan and volume. Here's a simple way to decide:

  • You already pay for a top X plan: use X Pro. It's native, handles threads and polls, and lives next to your timelines.
  • You run X ads: the ads.x.com Composer fits into tools you already use.
  • You post on several networks or want a free option: a third-party scheduler like Buffer or Typefully.
  • You post once a day at a time you're awake: you may not need scheduling. Keep drafts ready and post by hand.

Tips for scheduling posts that still feel human

Scheduling saves time, but the first half hour after a post goes live still matters. Plan to be around when your scheduled posts publish, at least for the important ones.

  • Schedule for your audience's time zone. Our guide to the best time to post on X for engagement shows how to find it.
  • Space posts out. Two to three hours apart gives each post its own window.
  • Check before big news. Pause cheerful scheduled posts if something serious is happening in your niche.
  • Reply in person. Scheduled posts plus live replies beat scheduled posts alone. Our tips on getting more replies on X help here.
  • Batch on one day. Write the week's posts in one sitting, then schedule them. A Twitter content calendar makes this easy.

Schedule smarter, not more

Learning how to schedule Twitter posts in advance frees you from the clock, so you can show up at your audience's best time without living on your phone. Pick one route, schedule next week's posts in a single sitting, and spend the time you save on replies. You've got this. New to all of this? Frequently Asked Questions

Can I schedule tweets for free?

Yes, you can schedule tweets for free with third-party tools. As of October 2026, Buffer's free plan connects up to three channels with 10 scheduled posts per channel, and Typefully also offers a free plan. X's native X Pro scheduler is listed as a Premium+ feature, so it needs a paid plan.

Can I schedule a whole thread in advance?

Yes, you can schedule a whole thread in advance with X Pro, whose composer creates threads and supports scheduled posts, according to X's Help Center. Buffer also says it can schedule X threads. Check that each post in the thread fits the 280 character limit before you schedule it.

Where do I find my scheduled posts on X?

Scheduled posts on X live in the tool you used to schedule them. In X Pro, add a Scheduled column to see your queue. In ads.x.com, open the Posts tab and switch the filter to Scheduled. Third-party tools like Buffer show scheduled posts in their own calendar.

Can I edit a scheduled post on X?

You can edit a scheduled post on X before it goes live in the tool you used. In ads.x.com, hover over the post, click the three dots and choose Edit. After publishing, X's help center says posts sent via X Pro can't be changed with the Edit post feature.

Do scheduled posts get less reach on X?

No official source says scheduled posts get less reach on X. X's open-source ranking code scores posts by predicted replies, reposts, likes and reading time, not by how they were sent. Reach drops when nobody is around to reply, so check in after important posts go live.
