How Does X Revenue Sharing Work? The 2026 Update

By Prashant Sharma · Oct 26, 2026 · 6 min read

How does X revenue sharing work?

X revenue sharing pays eligible creators based on how many verified users see their posts. As of October 2026, the old Creator Revenue Sharing program is retired, and Original Content Rewards replaced it. The new program pays every two weeks for qualified impressions, meaning views from Premium users, on original posts.

For a long time, "X revenue sharing" meant one program, and creators knew roughly how it worked. Then in August 2026, X closed the doors to new members. If you were planning to join, that change probably felt confusing. Let me explain what happened and how the money flows now.

Revenue sharing simply means X shares part of its income with the people whose posts keep users on the app. It matters because it's the only way X pays you just for posting, without you selling anything.

What happened to X Creator Revenue Sharing?

X retired Creator Revenue Sharing on September 7, 2026. New enrollments ended on August 7, 2026, according to X's Creator Revenue Sharing help page.

Members kept earning until the closing date. X listed three final payouts: August 14, August 28, and one for earnings through September 7, expected on or around September 11. From September 8, X began letting existing members apply to the new Original Content Rewards program.

Here's the part that surprises people. Moving over isn't automatic. Former members have to meet the new rules and apply. If your monetization was paused for a past policy violation, X says you can't enroll in the new program for now.

How did the old revenue sharing program work?

The old program rewarded Premium creators for posts that drove interactions and conversation, with earnings shaped by views from verified users. To join, creators needed an active Premium subscription, 5 million organic impressions in the last 3 months and 500 verified followers.

X never published a fixed rate. Its help page listed factors that shaped earnings instead:

  • Verified Home Timeline impressions: views from verified users in the main feed carried weight.
  • Who viewed the content: views from Premium+ subscribers could carry more value than views from Basic subscribers.
  • Content format: different formats could be weighted differently.

Payouts came every two weeks, with a $30 minimum, through Stripe or X Money. That basic rhythm carried over to the new program.

How does Original Content Rewards work?

Original Content Rewards pays eligible creators for qualified impressions on their original posts. A qualified impression is a unique view by a Premium user on the Home Timeline, where at least half the post is visible.

That definition comes from the Original Content Rewards help page, as of October 2026. In plain words, X pays for real people who pay for Premium seeing your work in their main feed. It doesn't describe a separate payment per like or reply. Paid, promoted and artificially generated impressions are excluded.

The word "original" does a lot of work here. X says your own take on news, your analysis or your expertise counts. Copied posts, lightly edited reuploads, compilations and content taken from other platforms don't.

What are the new eligibility rules?

The new entry bar is lower on impressions but still needs Premium and 500 verified followers. As of October 2026, X lists these main requirements:

  • Active Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business, or a qualifying plan on The X Subscription
  • 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days, not counting replies
  • 500 verified followers
  • 18 or older, based in a supported country, with an account in good standing
  • A Personal or Business account (political and government groups are excluded)

Meeting the rules doesn't guarantee approval. X reviews each application. The full list, including content rules, is in my guide to X creator monetization requirements.

How do payouts work in the new program?

Payouts arrive every two weeks once you pass $30, as of October 2026. U.S. creators get paid through X Money. Creators outside the U.S. connect a Stripe payout account and verify their identity through Stripe.

If you already finished ID verification and connected a payout method under the old program, X says you won't need to do those steps again. That's one less hurdle for returning creators.

What does this change mean for you?

For new creators, the change is good news. The impression target dropped from 5 million to 500,000, though now it counts only Home Timeline views from verified users. Your path to the program is shorter, as long as your work is clearly your own.

For accounts that leaned on reposted clips or meme compilations, it's a harder road. X has drawn a clear line around originality. Based on what I've seen, creators who share their own analysis and experience are in the best spot. Should you rely on X revenue sharing?

No, it's safer to treat X revenue sharing as one income stream, not your only one. X says it may modify or cancel the program at any time, and payouts aren't guaranteed.

The September 2026 switch proved that. Rules that worked for years changed in a few weeks. I'd pair rewards with something you control, like Creator Subscriptions, a service or a product. The short version

X revenue sharing still exists in spirit, but it now runs as Original Content Rewards. It pays for qualified views from Premium users on your original posts, every two weeks. The bar to enter is clearer and, for many creators, easier to reach. Focus on original, useful work, and the program becomes a nice reward for doing what already grows your account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is X Creator Revenue Sharing still available?

No, X Creator Revenue Sharing is no longer available. X stopped new enrollments on August 7, 2026, and retired the program on September 7, 2026. Its replacement is Original Content Rewards, which existing members could start applying for on September 8, 2026, if they meet the new eligibility rules.

Do I have to reapply for Original Content Rewards?

Yes, former Revenue Sharing members have to apply for Original Content Rewards and meet its rules. X says creators who already completed ID verification and connected a payout method won't need to repeat those steps. Accounts with monetization paused for a past violation can't enroll in the new program for now.

Does X revenue sharing pay for likes and replies?

No, X's current help page doesn't describe payments for likes or replies. Original Content Rewards pays for qualified impressions, which are unique views from Premium users on the Home Timeline with at least half the post visible. Likes and replies still help, because they spread posts to more readers.

When does X pay revenue sharing?

X pays Original Content Rewards every two weeks, as of October 2026, once earnings reach the $30 minimum. U.S. creators receive money through X Money. Creators in other supported countries use a Stripe payout account. The final payout from the old program was expected on or around September 11, 2026.

Can X stop paying creators?

Yes, X can stop or change creator payouts. X's help page says it may modify or cancel Original Content Rewards at any time, including for business, financial or legal reasons. X can also remove creators who break its rules, such as posting copied content or asking for engagement repeatedly.
