How to Increase Twitter Post Views (Before, During and After You Post)

By Prashant Sharma · Oct 26, 2026 · 5 min read

How do you increase Twitter post views?

To increase Twitter post views, write a first line that stops the scroll, use a format people engage with, and post when your followers are online. Then reply to every comment in the first hour. A view on X counts each time a logged-in person sees the post, so early reactions that spread it matter most.

Here's something that surprises a lot of people. Two posts with almost the same idea can end up far apart in views. The difference is rarely the idea. It's the first line, the timing, and what happens in the first hour.

This guide is about one post at a time. If you want to raise views across your whole account, how to get more impressions on X covers the bigger habits. Here, we'll take a single post from draft to done.

What counts as a view on X (formerly Twitter)?

A view on X is counted when anyone logged in sees your post, wherever they see it. That's straight from X's view counts help page. Followers and non-followers both count.

A few rules from that page are useful to know:

  • Your own views of your post count.
  • One person can add more than one view, for example on web and then on phone.
  • Posts embedded on other websites don't add views.
  • Community posts, Circle posts and older posts don't show view counts at all.
  • The count can take up to a minute to appear.

So views are about exposure. To raise them, you need X to put your post in front of more people, and that depends on how people react to it.

Before you post: shape the post for views

Most of a post's views are decided before you hit send. The first line, the format, and the length do most of the work.

Write the first line last

Draft the post, then rewrite the opening line. Put the most interesting part first: a number, a result, a surprise, or a clear opinion. If the first line could start any post, it's too weak.

Choose a format that fits the idea

Some formats tend to earn more reactions than others. Buffer's 2026 content format study found text posts had the highest median engagement rate on X, followed by images, then videos, with link posts last. For a closer look, see the best content formats for X growth.

Keep it tight

Short posts are easier to read in a fast feed. Run longer drafts through the free post length checker. If an idea really needs room, the thread maker turns it into a clean thread.

Give people something to answer

A post that asks for a reply tends to get one. A specific question beats "thoughts?" every time. "Which tool did you drop this year?" works. "Agree?" doesn't.

When you post: win the first hour

The first hour matters because early reactions help X decide whether to show the post to more people. X's open-source For You code also filters out posts older than 48 hours, so the window is short.

  • Post when your followers are online. Your first reactions come from people who already know you.
  • Reply to every early comment. Each reply adds to the conversation and gives people a reason to come back.
  • Don't post again right away. X's code gives each extra post from the same author a lower score in a viewer's feed, so a second post can steal views from the first.
  • Don't delete and repost. You lose the likes and replies you already earned.

After you post: give it a second life

A post that did well can earn more views later. You just need to put it back in front of people who missed it.

  • Quote it later. X's help page on reposting confirms you can repost or quote your own older posts when they're relevant again. Add a new line of context when you do.
  • Pin it. A pinned post sits at the top of your profile, so every new visitor sees it. See how to create a good pinned post.
  • Link to it in a relevant reply. Only when it actually answers someone's question. Dropping the same link everywhere looks like spam.

Why some posts still get few views

Sometimes a post gets almost no views even when it's good. That usually comes down to account size, timing, or topic match, not the post itself.

If this keeps happening, read why your tweets get no views. It walks through the less obvious causes, like protected accounts, posts in Communities, and account restrictions.

What this means for you

You don't need a big audience to get more views on a post. You need a strong first line, the right format, good timing, and a busy first hour. Those are all things you control.

One habit I'd add: compare each post's views to your own normal, not to someone else's. I built a free extension, XVision, that shows a post's views and how far it reached compared with the author's follower count. A post reaching well beyond your follower count is a clue about what to write next.

Better posts, better first hours

Increasing Twitter post views is mostly about craft and timing. Shape the post before you send it, stay with it for the first hour, and bring your best posts back later. Once you do this a few times, it becomes second nature.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do my own views count on X?

Yes, your own views count on X. The X help center says that if you are the author, looking at your own post counts as a view. Checking a post over and over adds a few views, but it won't change how the algorithm ranks the post, since ranking depends on how other people react.

Why do my views go up but likes stay at zero?

Views rising with zero likes on X means people saw the post but didn't find a reason to react. The first line may not deliver on its promise, or the topic may not fit the people seeing it. Try a sharper hook, a clearer point, or a direct question that invites a reply.

Do videos get more views than text on X?

Not necessarily. Buffer's 2026 content format study found text posts had the highest median engagement rate on X, at 3.56%, followed by images at 3.40% and videos at 2.96%. Video can still work well for ideas that need showing. Pick the format that fits the idea, then compare results on your own account.

Does editing a post reset its views on X?

X hasn't published how edits affect view counts or ranking, so there is no confirmed answer. Edit post needs X Premium or a paid X Subscription plan and allows up to 5 edits within 1 hour. The safest habit is to proofread before posting, so you don't need to edit during the post's most important first hour.

How many views is a good post on X?

A good view count on X depends on your size. Buffer found regular accounts typically got under 100 impressions per post, while Premium accounts got around 600. Measure each post against your own median. A post with double your usual views is a strong result, whatever the raw number is.
