How do you turn long text into an image for Twitter?

To turn long text into an image for Twitter, paste the text into a Twitter text to image converter, pick a theme, font and width such as 1080 pixels, and download the result. When the text is long, the converter splits it into several pages. X lets you attach up to 4 images to one post.

Some ideas don't fit in 280 characters, and a thread isn't always the right shape either. A short essay, a changelog or a list of lessons reads better as one or two clean pages. That is what this tool is for.

How to use the Twitter text to image converter

  1. Paste your text. Leave a blank line between paragraphs to add space between them.
  2. Pick a theme: Light, Dark, Paper or Bold.
  3. Choose the image width and page shape. Auto trims empty space so pages fit the text.
  4. Pick a font, then adjust font size and padding with the sliders.
  5. Add a footer, like your @handle, and keep page numbers on for multi-page notes.
  6. Check each page in the preview. Click Download on one page, or Download all.
  7. Click Copy text on each page and paste it into the image description on X.

What image sizes work best on X?

Images between 2:1 and 3:4 work best for a single photo on X. X's help page on posting photos and GIFs says a single photo in that range shows in full in the post, as of October 2026. The converter caps every page at 3:4, so a tall page never gets cut.

Why does alt text matter for text images?

Alt text matters because a screen reader can't read words inside an image. Without a description, people who are blind or have low vision get nothing from your note. X allows up to 1,000 characters per image description, and the steps are on X's page about making images accessible.

That is why each page has a Copy text button. Paste it into the image description. If a page has more than 1,000 characters, the tool tells you, so you can summarize it or use a bigger font to spread the text over more pages. The Twitter alt text generator helps with summaries.

Tips for readable text images

  • Keep the font big. People read these on phones. A font size of 36 to 44 at 1080 wide is a comfortable start.
  • Use short paragraphs. Blank lines become visible space, which makes a page feel lighter.
  • Aim for one to four pages. X allows four images per post. More than that belongs in a thread.
  • Say what it is in the post. A one-line summary in the post text tells people why to tap the images.

Example (made up): a founder shares "3 things I learned shipping our first product" as two Light pages at 1080 x 1440, with the handle in the footer and a one-line hook in the post.

Common mistakes

  • Tiny text. Fitting everything on one page makes it unreadable on a phone. Let it split.
  • No alt text. Text in an image is invisible to screen readers.
  • More than four pages in one post. X won't attach them. Split them across replies.
  • Pasting private details. Read the final image once before you post it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Twitter text to image converter free?

Yes, the Twitter text to image converter is free, with no sign-up and no watermark. You can make as many pages as you need and download them as PNG or JPEG files. Everything is drawn in your browser, so your text is never uploaded, stored or seen by anyone else.

How many images can I add to one post on X?

X lets you add up to 4 photos to one post, as listed in X's help center in October 2026. If your text needs five or more pages, post the first four and continue the rest in a reply, or trim the text until it fits on four pages.

Should I post text as an image or as a long post?

Post plain text when you can, because it is searchable, easy to quote and works for screen readers without extra steps. Use a text image when the layout matters, like a list or short essay you want people to see at a glance. Subscribers can also write longer posts far beyond 280 characters.

Why does my text split into several images?

The Twitter text to image converter splits text when it no longer fits on one page at your chosen font size, padding and page shape. Each page stays at or under a 3:4 ratio so X can show it in full. Make the font smaller, reduce padding or shorten the text for fewer pages.

Which format should I download, PNG or JPEG?

Choose PNG for text images because it keeps letters sharp with no blurry edges. JPEG files are smaller but can add faint smudges around text. X lists a 5MB limit for photos as of October 2026, and a PNG page of text from this tool is usually far below that.
