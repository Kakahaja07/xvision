Twitter video downloader

Save the video or GIF from a public X post as an MP4, in the quality you choose.

The downloader service isn't connected yet. The site owner needs to set up the free Cloudflare Worker described in worker/README.md.

Only download videos you made or have permission to use, and respect creators' rights. Works with public posts only; private, deleted or age-restricted posts can't be downloaded.

The post link is sent to the downloader service to look up the video. Nothing is stored.
