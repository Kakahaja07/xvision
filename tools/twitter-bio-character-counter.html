    How many characters can a Twitter bio have?

    A Twitter bio can have up to 160 characters, as of October 2026, according to the X Help Center. This Twitter bio character counter checks your text against that limit as you type. It shows how many characters remain and flags emoji, links and styled letters that may count as more than one.

    That sounds like plenty until you try to fit who you are, what you post and a reason to follow into one short block. I have rewritten my own bio many times, and a counter is where I start.

    How to use the Twitter bio character counter

    1. Type your bio in the box, or paste the one you have on X right now.
    2. Watch the big number. It shows your count out of 160, and the bar fills as you go.
    3. Check the status line. It says "Fits", "close to the limit" or "Over by" a number.
    4. Read the notes under the stats about emoji, links, hashtags and styled letters.
    5. If you are over, cut words yourself or press Trim to fit. It removes whole words from the end.
    6. Press Copy bio and paste it into your X profile.

    How does X count characters in a bio?

    X says a bio can be up to 160 characters, but its help pages do not explain how emoji, links or non-Latin letters count inside a bio. For posts, X does explain it. So this counter uses the post rules as a safe guide, then stays on the strict side.

    The X developer page on counting characters says most characters count as 1, while emoji and many other Unicode characters count as 2. Every link counts as 23 because X shortens it. The bio limit itself comes from X's page on how to customize your profile.

    Here's the thing. If your bio fits here, it should fit on X. If it is close to the limit, test it on X before you rely on it.

    What do the numbers mean?

    The big number is the strict count, and it is the one that matters for the 160 limit. Remaining is 160 minus that number. "Characters you see" counts each symbol once, the way you would by eye. "Post-style count" uses X's post rules exactly, with every link at 23.

    When "characters you see" and the big number differ, emoji or special letters are the reason. Each one quietly eats two spaces.

    Tips for a bio that earns the follow

    A strong bio answers one question fast: why should I follow you? You do not need all 160 characters to do that. Most good bios use three parts.

    • Who you help or what you do. "I help coffee shops look as good as their coffee."
    • One piece of proof. A role, a project, a number you can back up.
    • What people get. "Weekly posts on logos, packaging and pricing."

    Those lines are from a made-up designer, and they fit with room to spare. Press Try an example for more fictional bios.

    X's own search help says account search favors profiles with a complete name, username and bio. So an empty or half-done bio costs you more than looks. It can make you harder to find.

    Common bio mistakes to avoid

    Most bio problems come from trying to say everything at once. Each one is easy to fix.

    • Keyword lists. "Designer. Founder. Dad. Runner. Coffee." tells people facts but no reason to follow.
    • Too many hashtags. X suggests no more than 2 per post. A bio stuffed with tags looks like spam.
    • Fancy fonts. Styled letters count as 2, can confuse screen readers and may not show up in search.
    • Long links in the bio text. Put your main link in the Website field so the bio stays readable.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do emojis count as 2 characters in a Twitter bio?

    Emojis count as 2 characters in X posts, according to X's developer page on counting characters. X does not say whether bios use the same rule. This Twitter bio character counter counts each emoji as 2 to stay safe, so a bio that fits here should also fit inside X's 160-character limit.

    Does a link in my Twitter bio count as 23 characters?

    Links in X posts count as 23 characters because X shortens them. The X Help Center does not say how links count in a bio. This counter uses 23 or the link's real length, whichever is longer. Putting your main link in the separate Website field keeps the 160-character bio free for words.

    Can I make my Twitter bio longer than 160 characters?

    No, the X Help Center lists 160 characters as the bio limit as of October 2026, and the X help pages checked for this tool do not mention a longer bio on paid plans. If you need more space, use the Website field for a link, a pinned post for detail, and keep the bio itself short and clear.

    Do line breaks count toward the Twitter bio limit?

    Yes, a line break is a character, and this counter counts each one as 1, the same way X counts them in posts. Two line breaks to space out three short lines cost 2 characters. That is usually worth it if the bio becomes easier to scan on a phone.

    Does my display name count toward the 160 bio characters?

    No, your display name is a separate field with its own limit of 50 characters, and your @username has a limit of 15. Location and website are separate fields too. Only the text in the bio box counts toward the 160-character bio limit on X, according to the X Help Center.
