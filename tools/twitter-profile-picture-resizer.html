What size should a Twitter profile picture be?

A Twitter profile picture should be 400 x 400 pixels, the size X recommends, saved as a JPG, PNG or GIF under 2MB. This free Twitter profile picture resizer crops any photo into that square, shows a circle preview, and exports a 400 x 400 PNG or JPEG right in your browser.

Your profile photo shows up next to every post, reply and DM you send. It's tiny in most places, so a photo that looks great full size can turn into a blurry blob. Getting the crop right takes a minute and pays off every time someone sees your name.

How to use the Twitter profile picture resizer

  1. Drop a photo onto the box, or click it to choose one from your device.
  2. Drag the photo inside the square to move it. Use the Zoom slider to get closer.
  3. Fine-tune with the Left / right and Up / down sliders if dragging feels fiddly on a phone.
  4. Check the two round previews. The small one is close to how you look in a busy feed.

PNG or JPEG: which format should you pick?

Pick PNG for logos, illustrations and anything with flat colors or sharp text. Pick JPEG for regular photos of people or places.

PNG keeps edges crisp and supports transparency. JPEG makes smaller files for photos, but it has no transparency, so the tool fills empty areas with the color you choose. A quality around 90% is a good place to start for headshots. If your logo has a transparent background, keep in mind that X may show it on a light or dark theme, so a solid background color is often the safer choice.

Tips for a profile photo people recognize

A good profile photo is a clear face or a simple logo that still reads at the size of a thumbnail. Here's what I look for:

  • Fill the circle. Zoom in until your face takes up most of the space. Head and shoulders is plenty.
  • Keep the corners empty. Most profiles show the photo as a circle, so anything in the corners gets cut.
  • Use contrast. A plain background behind you makes your face stand out at small sizes.
  • Check the tiny preview. If you can't tell it's you at 40 pixels, zoom in more.
  • Use a real photo of you. X's authenticity policy lists stock or stolen profile photos, used to deceive, as signs of a fake persona.

Common profile picture mistakes

The most common mistake is uploading a wide group or landscape photo and letting X crop the middle. You end up as a speck in the center. Cropping it yourself avoids that.

  • Uploading a tiny image, like 100 x 100, and stretching it. Start with a photo at least 400 pixels on its short side.
  • Putting text in a personal photo. It's unreadable at feed size.
  • Changing the photo often. People recognize you by your photo before your name. If you have a blue checkmark, X also removes it for a while after a photo change until the account is reviewed again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my Twitter profile picture blurry?

A Twitter profile picture usually looks blurry when the original photo is too small or was cropped from a tiny part of a bigger image. Start with a photo at least 400 pixels on its shortest side, crop it to 400 x 400 with this resizer, and avoid zooming in so far that the source runs out of detail.

Is my Twitter profile picture a circle or a square?

Most accounts on X show the profile picture as a circle, but you always upload a square image. That's why this tool crops to a 400 x 400 square and shows a round preview. Keep your face or logo in the middle and leave the corners plain, since the circle cuts them off.

Does this profile picture resizer upload my photo?

No, this profile picture resizer never uploads your photo. The file is read, cropped and resized by your own browser using a canvas element, and the download is created on your device. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the tool will keep working.

Can I use a GIF as my X profile picture?

You can upload a GIF file as your X profile picture, but X doesn't support animated GIFs for profile photos, so only a still image will show. This tool reads a GIF's first frame and saves it as a PNG or JPEG, which is the simplest way to get a clean result.

What is the maximum file size for an X profile photo?

The maximum file size for an X profile photo is 2MB, according to X's help center as of October 2026. A 400 x 400 image from this tool is usually well under that limit. The tool shows the exact file size before you download, and warns you if a file goes over 2MB.
