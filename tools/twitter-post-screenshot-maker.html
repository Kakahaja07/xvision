How do I make a Twitter post screenshot?

To make a Twitter post screenshot, type your name, @handle and post text into the free Twitter Post Screenshot Maker, add an optional photo, date and metrics, pick a light, dim or dark theme, and download a clean PNG. It is built for mockups and for sharing your own posts.

A real screenshot often drags in clutter: a notification badge, a half-cut reply, the wrong theme. This tool draws a clean image from scratch, so the result is sharp and tidy every time.

How to use the Twitter Post Screenshot Maker

  1. Type your display name and @handle. The handle field keeps only letters, numbers and underscores, up to 15 characters, like X does.
  2. Write or paste the post text. Line breaks are kept, and links, @mentions and #hashtags turn blue.
  3. Add a profile photo if you like. Without one, the tool draws your initials in a circle.
  4. Set the date and time shown under the post.
  5. Tick Show metrics to add replies, reposts, likes and views. Big numbers shorten to forms like 12.4K.
  6. Pick Light, Dim or Dark, a card width, padding and a background color.
  7. Press Download PNG. The image is saved at twice the size for crisp text.

When is a post mockup useful?

A post mockup is useful when you want to show your own post outside X, or plan one before it goes live. Think slides, blog posts, case studies, portfolios and design reviews.

  • Share your best post on another platform without the app's buttons around it.
  • Show a client or teammate how a launch post will read before it is published.
  • Add a past post to a talk or course slide in your brand's colors.

The live counter under the text box uses X's weighting rules, so you'll know if the text fits in 280 characters. Links count as 23, and emoji count as 2.

Is it OK to make a fake post for someone else?

No. Making a post image that puts words in a real person's mouth is misleading, and sharing it on X can break X's rules. X's Authenticity policy bans impersonation and media shared as real that has been fabricated or misquoted in harmful ways.

You can read the full text in X's Authenticity policy. Its section on synthetic and manipulated media covers fabricated media that depicts a real person and media shown with misquotes or false claims. That is exactly what a fake post screenshot is.

That's also why this tool leaves some things out on purpose.

Tips for a clean post image

  • Use Standard width for most slides. Pick Narrow for phone-shaped layouts like stories.
  • Match the theme to where the image goes. Dark cards pop on light slides, and light cards work on dark ones.
  • Give it room. Padding of 40 to 60 pixels with a soft background looks calm and balanced.
  • Keep metrics honest. If you show numbers, copy them from the real post.
  • Use a square profile photo. The tool crops it to a circle from the center.

Here's an example. Leo, a fictional indie developer, wants his launch post on his landing page. He types his real post, adds his photo, picks Dim with 48 pixels of padding on a navy background, and drops the PNG into his site.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Pasting a post that is over 280 characters when your account can't post it. Check the counter.
  • Using a tiny, blurry profile photo. Start with at least 400 x 400 pixels.
  • Inventing big numbers. Fake metrics mislead readers and hurt trust when someone checks.
  • Exporting a dark card onto a dark page. Use padding and a contrasting background.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make a screenshot of someone else's post with this tool?

The Twitter Post Screenshot Maker is meant for your own posts and design mockups. To share someone else's post, take a real screenshot of it or link to it on X so people see the original. Creating a post image with words a real person never wrote is misleading and can break X's rules.

Why is there no verified checkmark option?

The Twitter Post Screenshot Maker has no verified checkmark because a badge implies X has reviewed the account, and a mockup can't show that. On X, the blue checkmark means an active paid subscription that met X's review rules. Leaving it out keeps every exported image honest about what it is.

What size is the exported PNG?

The exported PNG is twice the size of the card you see, so text stays sharp on high-resolution screens. A Standard 600 pixel card with 40 pixels of padding exports at 1360 pixels wide. The height grows with your text, and the tool shows the exact size under the Download button.

Does the screenshot maker upload my photo or text?

No, the Twitter Post Screenshot Maker never uploads anything. Your text and profile photo are drawn on a canvas inside your browser, and the PNG is created on your own device. Nothing is stored, so you can mock up unreleased launch posts or client work without sharing them.

Can the post text be longer than 280 characters?

Yes, the Twitter Post Screenshot Maker accepts longer text, and the counter warns you when it passes 280. Standard X posts are limited to 280 characters as of October 2026. Longer posts are only available on paid plans, so only show long text if your account can really post it.
