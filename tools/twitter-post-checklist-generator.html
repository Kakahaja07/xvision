What is a Twitter post checklist generator?

A Twitter post checklist generator builds a pre-publish checklist for the exact kind of X post you are about to send. This free tool covers single posts, threads, launches, image posts, link posts and replies, auto-checks a pasted draft for length, hashtags, links and caps, and gives you a readiness score.

Pilots use checklists even after thousands of flights. Not because they forget how to fly, but because small misses are easy when you are in a hurry. Posting is the same. The typo, the broken link or the missing alt text usually happens when you are rushing to hit post.

How to use the post checklist generator

  1. Pick your post type. The checklist changes for each one.
  2. Paste your draft if you want automatic checks. For a thread, leave a blank line between posts.
  3. Read the auto results. "Auto" means the check passed, "Fix" means it needs work.
  4. Tick the manual items as you check them, like proofreading and timing.
  5. Watch the readiness score. At 100%, you are ready to post.
  6. Click "Copy checklist" to save it in your notes, or "Reset ticks" to start over.

What does the tool check automatically?

The tool runs six automatic checks on your draft. They catch the mechanical problems, so you can spend your attention on the idea itself.

The readiness score is simple: checked items divided by total items for that post type. A link post has a different list than a reply, so the scores aren't meant to be compared across types.

Which X rules are built into the checklist?

The checklist follows X's own guidance where it exists, as of October 2026. Where X has no rule, the item is a best practice, and the hint says so.

  • 280 characters. That is the standard post limit, and one post can hold up to 4 photos, a GIF or a video, according to X's How to Post page.
  • 2 hashtags at most. X recommends "using no more than 2 hashtags per post as best practice" on its hashtag help page. You can use more, but it rarely helps.
  • Threads get shortened. X truncates threads of 4 or more posts in timelines with a "Show this thread" option, so post 1 has to earn the click.
  • Links are disputed. X's head of product said in April 2026 that link posts are not penalized and advised giving context before the link. The checklist asks for that context.

Timing is the fuzziest item. Buffer's 2026 data points to weekday mornings, around 9 to 11 a.m. local time, while Sprout Social's points to 12 to 6 p.m. Both rank Saturday as weak. Test your own audience.

Tips and a quick example

Here's a fictional example. Sam drafts a link post about a budgeting guide: "NEW GUIDE!!! Read it here #money #budget #finance #savings" plus a link. The tool flags 4 hashtags, 2 all-caps words and almost no context before the link. Sam rewrites it to say what the guide covers, cuts to one hashtag, and ends with a question. The fixes show up in the score right away.

  • Use the checklist for important posts. Launches, threads and link posts gain the most.
  • Read the draft on a phone. Most people will see it there.
  • Edit with care. With a paid plan you can edit an original post up to 5 times within 1 hour, but threads and replies can't be edited.

What are common pre-publish mistakes?

The most common mistake is a weak first line. If line one is "So I've been thinking...", most people scroll past before the good part.

  • Stuffing hashtags into the end of the post.
  • Posting an image with no alt text.
  • Pasting a link with no reason to click it.
  • Posting and leaving right away, with no time to reply.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I check before posting on Twitter?

Before posting on Twitter, check that the first line works alone, the post has one clear idea, it fits 280 characters, it uses 2 hashtags or fewer, images have alt text, links have context, and the text is proofread. This checklist generator adds items for threads, launches, image posts, link posts and replies.

How many hashtags should a post on X have?

A post on X should have no more than 2 hashtags, which is X's own best-practice advice on its hashtag help page. You can add more, but extra hashtags make a post harder to read, and X's rules count excessive, unrelated hashtags as spam. Many strong posts use none.

How is the readiness score calculated?

The readiness score is the number of checked items divided by the total items for your post type, shown as a percentage. Auto checks tick or untick themselves when you paste a draft. Manual items, like proofreading and timing, count only when you tick them yourself.

Does the checklist save my draft?

No, the post checklist generator does not save your draft. The checks run in your browser tab, nothing is uploaded, and ticks reset when you close the page. Click "Copy checklist" if you want to keep the list in your notes app or share it with a teammate.

Should I put links in the post or in a reply?

X's head of product said in April 2026 that link posts are not penalized and suggested putting the link in the post with clear context. The topic is still disputed, so test both on your own account. Either way, tell readers what they will get before they click.
