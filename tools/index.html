Free tools

Small tools for writing on X

They run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

X video downloader

Save the video or GIF from a public X post as an MP4, in the quality you choose.

Thread maker

Paste long writing and split it into a numbered X thread, with every post under the limit.

Post length checker

Count characters the way X does: links count as 23, emoji as 2.

Engagement rate calculator

See what share of viewers liked, replied or reposted, and how a post compares.
