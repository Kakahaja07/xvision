How do you calculate Twitter profile conversion rate?

A Twitter profile conversion calculator divides your new followers by your profile visits and multiplies by 100. If 1,250 people visited your profile and 45 followed you, your conversion rate is 3.6%, or one follower for every 28 visits. X publishes no benchmark for this rate, so compare it with your own past periods.

This one number tells you how well your profile turns curiosity into follows. Your posts bring people to the door. Your profile decides whether they come in.

How to use the profile conversion calculator

  1. Find profile visits and new followers for one period in your X analytics. Use the same date range for both.
  2. Type them in. Add impressions for that period if you have them.
  3. Read your conversion rate and visits per new follower.
  4. Change "Improve the rate by" to see how many extra followers a better profile could bring from the same visits.
  5. Add last period's numbers to see whether you are improving.

Nothing leaves your browser. The tool does not connect to X.

The formulas and a worked example

The main formula is new followers ÷ profile visits × 100. Two related rates help you find where people drop off.

  • Profile visit rate = profile visits ÷ impressions × 100. Are your posts making people curious about you?
  • Follow rate = new followers ÷ impressions × 100. X's ads glossary uses "follow rate" for new followers compared to impressions.

Is there a benchmark for profile conversion rate?

No verified public benchmark exists, so this calculator doesn't show one. X doesn't publish an average, and figures you may see online rarely explain where they came from.

Your own history is a better yardstick anyway. A news commentator and a niche software developer attract very different visitors. Track your rate every month and judge changes against your own past.

What to fix when your conversion rate is low

A low rate usually means visitors can't tell, in a few seconds, what you post and why they should care. Work through these in order.

  1. Bio. Say who you help or what you post about. You have 160 characters, per X's profile help page. The bio generator gives you drafts.
  2. Pinned post. X lets you pin one post as the first thing visitors see. Pin your best post or a short intro. Try the pinned post generator.
  3. Header and photo. X recommends 1500 x 500 pixels for the header and 400 x 400 for the photo. A clear face or logo and a header that states your topic help. The header maker builds one at the right size.
  4. Recent posts. Visitors scroll. If your last few posts are off-topic replies, they don't see why to follow.
  5. Match. If a post brings visitors about one topic, your profile should show more of that topic.

For a full check, run the profile audit. The guide on how to turn profile visits into followers goes deeper, and the guide on how to create a good Twitter pinned post covers the pin in detail.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing date ranges. Visits from 7 days and followers from 28 days give a meaningless rate.
  • Reading one viral day. A big post can bring thousands of visitors who don't share your interests, so your rate drops even though the profile didn't change.
  • Treating every follow as a profile follow. People can follow from a post or a recommendation without visiting your profile. Treat the rate as a close estimate.
  • Changing everything at once. Change one thing per period so you know what worked.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good profile conversion rate on X?

X does not publish a good profile conversion rate, and no verified public benchmark exists as of October 2026. The best test is your own trend. If your rate is higher this month than last month at similar traffic, your profile is getting better at turning visitors into followers. Check it every 30 days.

Where do I find profile visits on Twitter?

Profile visits appear in X analytics, both for your whole account and for each post. Reports say full account analytics need a paid plan as of October 2026, while a single post's analytics view shows profile visits and new followers. Use the same date range for visits and new followers in this calculator.

Why is my conversion rate over 100%?

A rate over 100% means you gained more followers than you had profile visits. Some people follow you straight from a post or from a recommendation without opening your profile. It happens most with small numbers or after a popular post. Use a longer period, such as 28 days, for a steadier reading.

Does a higher profile conversion rate help reach?

A higher profile conversion rate does not directly raise the reach of your posts, but it grows your audience faster from the same traffic. X's open-source ranking code, as of October 2026, gives the predicted chance of a viewer following the author a weight of 4.0, a strong positive signal.
