What is a Twitter product announcement generator?

A Twitter product announcement generator turns product news into ready-to-edit posts for X. This free tool takes your product name, a one-line summary and your changelog bullets, then writes 6 announcement drafts and a short thread for a new feature, update, pricing change, milestone or sunset, with a character count on each.

Shipping is the hard part. Telling people about it should be the easy part, yet many teams ship a great feature and announce it with one flat line. Or they skip the post and hope users notice. This tool gives you several angles in seconds, so you can pick the one that fits your news.

How to use the product announcement generator

  1. Pick what you are announcing: new feature, update, pricing change, milestone or sunset.
  2. Pick a tone: plain, friendly, excited or formal.
  3. Enter your product name and the news in one short line, like "custom changelog templates".
  4. Paste your changelog bullets, one per line. Dashes and numbers at the start are removed for you.
  5. Add a date for pricing changes or sunsets, and a link if you have release notes.
  6. Copy the draft you like, fill in any [bracketed] parts, and click "Generate again" for new openers.

What do the announcement types change?

Each type changes the opening line, the list heading, the closing question and the details users need. A sunset needs different words than a launch, and a pricing change needs more care than a bug fix.

TypeWhat the drafts focus on
New featureWhat users can do now and what they will try first
UpdateWhat changed, what got faster or fixed
Pricing changeThe new price, the start date and what happens to current customers
MilestoneThanks to users and what comes next
SunsetThe end date and the one step users must take

The excited tone is turned off for pricing changes and sunsets on purpose. Nobody wants a cheerful post about paying more or losing a tool they use.

What X limits affect an announcement?

The main limit is length. A standard X post can be up to 280 characters and hold up to 4 photos, a GIF or a video, according to X's How to Post help page, as of October 2026. Subscribers can write much longer posts, but many readers still see a cut-off preview.

That is why each draft shows a live count, and the thread card shows its longest post. This tool counts each link as 23 characters.

Spotted a typo after posting? With X Premium or a paid X Subscription plan, you can edit an original post up to 5 times within 1 hour, according to X's Edit post page. Threads and replies can't be edited, so check those twice.

Tips for better product announcements

Good announcements talk about the user's result, not your work. "You can now preview before you publish" lands better than "We refactored the publish flow".

  • Lead with the change in plain words. If someone reads only line one, they should know what is new.
  • Keep bullets short. Three to five bullets fit in one post. More than that reads better as a thread.
  • Show it. A screenshot or 10-second clip explains a feature faster than text.
  • Be direct about bad news. For pricing and sunsets, say the date, who is affected and what to do.

Here's a fictional example. A small team behind a note app called "Shipnote" adds custom templates. They pick "New feature" and "Friendly", paste three bullets, and get a short headline post, a changelog list, a version that ends with a question, and a 5-post thread.

What are common announcement mistakes?

The most common mistake is writing for your team instead of your users. Internal names, version numbers and ticket titles mean nothing to most readers.

  • Hiding a price increase in a long list of features.
  • Announcing a sunset with no date and no next step.
  • Posting the update once and never again. People miss posts, so share it in a follow-up later in the week.
  • Adding a pile of hashtags. X itself recommends no more than 2 per post.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you announce a new feature on Twitter?

Announce a new feature on Twitter with one clear line about what users can do now, a screenshot or short clip, and a link to details. Keep the post under 280 characters. This generator writes six versions plus a short thread from your feature name and changelog bullets, so you can pick the best one.

How do you announce a price increase on X without upsetting users?

Announce a price increase on X early, plainly and with the facts users need: the new price, the start date, the reason, and what happens to current customers. Avoid hype words. The pricing change option in this generator adds placeholders for each of those details so you don't forget one.

Should a changelog go in one post or a thread?

A changelog with up to about three or four short items fits in one X post of 280 characters. Longer changelogs read better as a thread, with one change per post and a screenshot. The generator gives you both a single-post list and a short thread, with a count on every draft.

How often should a product account post updates?

A product account should post an update whenever users can notice a change, plus a follow-up later that week for people who missed it. There is no official X rule on frequency. Steady weekly or biweekly update posts help users see that the product is alive and improving.

Does the product announcement generator use AI?

No, the product announcement generator does not use AI. It fills written templates with your product name, news, bullets, date and link, and picks openers and closers to match your tone. It runs fully in your browser, so your unreleased news is never uploaded anywhere.
