How do you come up with content ideas for X?

The easiest way to find content ideas for X is to mix your niche with proven post formats: lessons, stories, lists, opinions, behind the scenes, questions and threads. This free Twitter content ideas generator does that for you, giving 18 specific ideas for your niche, audience and goal, each with a one-line angle.

Blank-page days happen to everyone who posts often. I noticed that when I'm stuck, it's rarely because I have nothing to say. It's because I'm trying to invent a whole post from nothing. Picking a format first, then filling it with my own experience, gets me unstuck in minutes.

How to use the Twitter Content Ideas Generator

What are the seven post formats?

A post format is the shape of a post, separate from its topic. The same topic can become seven very different posts depending on the format.

FormatWhat it isGood for
LessonOne thing you learned, with the moment you learned itTeaching and trust
StorySomething that happened to you, told with real detailsConnection
ListTools, mistakes or tips, one per lineSaves and quick value
OpinionA clear view on a debate in your nicheReplies and reach
Behind the scenesYour process, setup or numbersTrust and curiosity
QuestionAn open question your audience can answerConversations
ThreadSeveral connected posts that go deep on one ideaDepth and authority

Threads have one quirk worth knowing. X's thread help page says threads of four or more posts are cut off on timelines with a "Show this thread" link, so your first post has to earn the click.

Which formats work best on X?

No single format wins for everyone, so test a few and watch your own numbers. There is some outside data. A 2026 Buffer study of content formats found that text posts on X had the highest median engagement rate (3.56%), ahead of images (3.40%), videos (2.96%) and link posts (2.25%).

That fits with a text-first tool like this one. Still, it's one study of Buffer users' posts, and your audience may behave differently. Treat it as a starting point, not a rule.

Tips for turning an idea into a post

An idea becomes a good post when you add something only you know. Here is a made-up example. The idea "A small win that kept me going" in a fictional baking niche becomes: "Sold my first loaf to a stranger at the Saturday market. $6. I almost didn't set up the table that morning."

  • Add one real detail. A number, a date or a quote turns a generic idea into your post.
  • Follow the angle. The grey line under each idea tells you how to open or structure it.
  • Batch your ideas. Copy all 18 into a notes app and pick from that list all week.
  • Repeat formats that work. If your stories get more replies than your lists, write more stories.

Common mistakes with content ideas

The most common mistake is posting the idea as-is, with no personal detail. "5 tools I use every week" only works if you name the tools and say why.

  • Only one format. Ten lists in a row feel repetitive, even if each one is good.
  • Ideas for everyone. The audience box matters. "Tips for freelancers" beats "tips for people".
  • Waiting for the perfect idea. A decent idea posted today teaches you more than a great one in drafts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Twitter content ideas generator free?

Yes, the XVision Twitter Content Ideas Generator is free with no sign-up and no limit. It runs in your browser, so your niche and audience are never uploaded. You can generate new sets of 18 ideas as many times as you like and copy any of them into your notes or drafts.

Does the content ideas generator use AI?

No, the content ideas generator uses a fixed bank of post templates written by hand, mixed with your niche and audience. It picks two ideas per format plus four that match your goal. Because it doesn't use AI, it won't invent facts, but you need to add your own details.

How many ideas do I need for a month on X?

That depends on how often you post. At one post a day you need about 30 ideas a month, so two rounds of this generator cover most of it. Buffer's consistency research found that creators who posted in more weeks got more engagement per post, so plan a pace you can keep.

Can I use the same idea more than once?

Yes, you can reuse an idea if you change the format or the details. A lesson can come back later as a story, a list or a thread. Avoid posting the same text again, though. X's spam rules cover repeatedly posting identical or nearly identical posts.
