How do you make a good poll on X?

A good poll on X asks one clear question and offers 2 to 4 short choices that cover the real options. X polls allow up to 4 choices of 25 characters each and can run from 5 minutes to 7 days. This free Twitter poll generator writes the question and choices for you, already inside those limits.

Polls are one of the easiest posts to answer. Nobody has to think of a reply, they just tap. But I've seen plenty of polls fall flat because the choices were vague or cut off. Most of the work is in the wording, and that's the part this tool speeds up.

How to use the Twitter Poll Generator

  1. Type your topic, like "remote work" or "home espresso". For a this-or-that poll you can also type "Notion vs Obsidian".
  2. Pick a poll type: This or that, Opinion scale, Prediction or Preference.
  3. Add your own choices, separated by commas, if you already know them.
  4. Choose how long the poll should run.
  5. Press Generate polls. The first poll loads into the editor, and six more ideas appear underneath.
  6. Edit the question and choices. Each choice shows a live count and stops at 25 characters.
  7. Copy the full poll, then paste the question into X's compose box and each choice into its own field.

Which poll type should you pick?

Pick the poll type that matches what you want to learn. Each type asks a different kind of question.

  • This or that: two clear options, like two tools or two habits. Fast to answer and easy to argue about in the replies.
  • Opinion scale: agree or disagree in steps. Good for testing a take before you write a full post about it.
  • Prediction: where people think a topic is going. Fun to quote later when you know the answer.
  • Preference: how people actually do something, like when they work or how they learn. This is the most useful type for research.

Poll tips and examples

Write choices that don't overlap. Here is a made-up example from a fictional coffee account. Weak: "How often do you drink coffee?" with "A lot", "Sometimes", "Daily", "Rarely". "A lot" and "Daily" overlap. Better: "None", "1 cup a day", "2 to 3 cups", "4 or more".

  • Use the fourth choice wisely. "Something else (reply)" turns quiet voters into repliers.
  • Match the length to the question. A quick reaction poll can run a few hours. A research poll you want many people to see can run longer.
  • Reply with the results. When the poll ends, share what you learned. It gives voters a reason to come back.

Common poll mistakes

The most common mistake is a choice that gets cut off or reads oddly because it was squeezed into 25 characters at the last second. Write short choices first, then the question.

  • Leading questions. "Isn't it obvious that remote work is better?" tells people the answer you want.
  • Missing options. If a common answer isn't listed, people skip the poll.
  • Two questions in one. "Do you like coffee and tea?" can't be answered with one tap.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Twitter poll generator free?

Yes, the XVision Twitter Poll Generator is free with no sign-up and no limit on how many polls you create. It runs in your browser, so your topics and choices are never uploaded. Generate as many ideas as you like, edit one, and copy it into X when it looks right.

How many characters can a poll choice have on X?

A poll choice on X can have up to 25 characters, according to X's help page as of October 2026. That includes spaces and punctuation. This poll generator counts every choice live, shortens generated choices at a word break, and stops you from typing past 25 characters in the editor.

Can I add a photo to an X poll?

No, X's help page says photos can't be included in a poll. If you need a visual, post the image first and add the poll in a reply, or describe the options in the question text. Keep the choices themselves short, because each one is limited to 25 characters.

Can people see how I voted in a poll?

No, votes in X polls are private. X's help page says neither the poll creator nor other voters can see who voted or how they voted. People see the current results after they vote, and the winning choice is shown in bold when the poll ends.

How long should an X poll run?

An X poll can run anywhere from 5 minutes to 7 days, and it defaults to 1 day. There is no official best length. A short poll fits a live moment, like an event you're watching. A longer poll gives people in other time zones a chance to see it and vote.
